real_speech.wav
  Three sentences of real, close-miked speech (28 seconds, 22.05 kHz, 16-bit mono), used by the
  tests as a reality check next to the synthetic voices. They are clips LJ001-0001, -0003 and
  -0005 of the LJ Speech Dataset (Keith Ito and Linda Johnson, https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/),
  which is in the public domain; a third of a second of room tone was put between them.
